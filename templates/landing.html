End-to-end encrypted

Answers are sealed with AES-256-GCM in the respondent's browser via the Web Crypto API. Plaintext never leaves their device.

PKI-powered

A built-in Certificate Authority signs every user's RSA public key into an X.509 certificate, so respondents always encrypt to the genuine owner — never an impostor.

Zero plaintext storage

The server stores ciphertext, wrapped keys and password-encrypted keystores only. Even a full database breach reveals nothing readable.
